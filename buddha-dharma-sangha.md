One of the fundamental messages or #zen , the way I understand it, is that the individual is limited, *you* are limited. Your intellect is limited, your will is weak, your body is fragile too. You cannot grasp #zen by your own, you cannot achieve enlightenment on your own, you cannot even break a bad habit on your own.

However, acting as an agent of a bigger community (sangha) frees you from all those limitations.

An individual sees things in an individualistic viewpoint, biased if you will. A viewpoint in which the things that are out of sight of the individual are obscured (a rich person does not see the problems the poor people have, but the poor people don't see the problems rich people have, as well). 

Because of these limitations, an individual cannot grasp the fundamental nature of reality (dharma). But a community, which makes an conscious effort can see it. And you can see it, through the community, (although that is not completely true, because in true community there is no "you").


When people remove our biases, our weaknesses, we become awake (buddha), we become more than manipulators, more than mere spectators, we become true part of the universe.

This knowledge is essential, but at the same time completely useless by itself. So, if you want to realize it, find some people to practice it with and form a sangha.
